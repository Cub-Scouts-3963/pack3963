Pack 3963 Fundraising

Popcorn Powers Our Adventures

Fund the fun—and build confidence

Popcorn sales help Pack 3963 provide activities, advancement materials, recognition, event supplies, and memorable experiences for our Scouts. Fundraising also gives Scouts a chance to practice goal-setting, communication, responsibility, and teamwork.

Shop and support a Scout

Option 1

Sell Online

  1. Look for the Pecatonica River Popcorn invitation sent to a parent or guardian.
  2. Use the Scout’s Seller ID to create or access the family account.
  3. Share the Scout’s personal store link with friends and family.
  4. Customers order online and their purchases ship directly to them.

If you cannot find the invitation or Seller ID, email {{ site.data.pack.email }} before creating a second account.

Open the Scout seller portal

Option 2

Sell In Person

  1. Get the current order form and Pack instructions from your den leader.
  2. Record each customer’s order and payment carefully.
  3. Return forms and money by the deadline announced by Pack leadership.
  4. Pick up products when the Pack shares the delivery schedule, then deliver promptly.

Dates, accepted payment methods, and pickup instructions may change each season. Use the latest Pack email or contact {{ site.data.pack.email }} before collecting orders.

Sell Safely

  • Scouts should always sell with a parent, guardian, or approved adult.
  • Use the buddy system and remain in familiar, well-lit areas.
  • Never enter a customer’s home or vehicle.
  • Keep cash secure and give it to an adult promptly.
  • Be courteous, accept “no thank you,” and thank every person for their time.

Before You Sell

Set a Goal

  • Choose a realistic personal sales goal.
  • Practice a short introduction and explain what the fundraiser supports.
  • Confirm the Scout’s Seller ID and online store link.
  • Wear the Scout uniform when appropriate.

After the Sale

Finish Strong

  • Double-check order forms, customer details, and payments.
  • Turn everything in on time using the current Pack instructions.
  • Keep customers informed about delivery timing.
  • Watch for current prize and recognition information from Pack leadership.

Questions or Current Sale Details?

Contact Pack 3963 at {{ site.data.pack.email }} for Seller ID help, Pack deadlines, order-form collection, payment, pickup, or prize information.
