Before publishing public content

  1. Confirm Pack contact and meeting details are accurate.
  2. Keep exactly one upcoming event marked as featured.
  3. Use JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP images with clear accessibility descriptions.
  4. Do not include a child's full name or private family information.
  5. Confirm that the Pack has permission to publish each photo.
  6. Save the update and confirm the automated site checks pass.

Website-manager content is public after publication. The treasurer portal is private and available only to approved email accounts.
